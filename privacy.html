Privacy Policy

Terms of Destruction · Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what the Terms of Destruction game ("the game", "we") collects when you play it on iOS, Android or the web, and what we do with it. The short version: your progress stays on your device, we don't ask who you are, and the only data that leaves your device is what the store, ads and anonymous game statistics need.

What stays on your device

Your progress, Cash, unlocks, settings and season progress are saved on your device only. We don't have an account system and we can't see your save. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this game. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive or to double a reward). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS we ask your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you say no, you still get ads, just not personalized ones. In the EU, UK and other regions that require it, we show Google's consent form first. See how Google uses data from ads. Buying "No Ads" removes the ad offers.

Game statistics

We may collect anonymous gameplay events (for example: a run started, a level was cleared, a purchase was made) to balance the game and fix problems. These events are not linked to your name, email or contacts.

Leaderboards

If you take part in the weekly leaderboard, we store the three-letter tag you choose, the hero you used and your score. Don't use your real name as your tag.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in your phone's settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Delete the app to delete your save.
  • Ask us to delete leaderboard entries or any other data, using the contact below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
